## Stage 47 delivery summary

1. **All 84 days × 4 compulsory skills = 336 in-app resource slots** are retained and surfaced in the app. Reading passages/questions, Listening MP3/script/questions, Writing Task 1 visuals/Task 2 prompts, and Speaking Part 1/2/3 content remain the required core. 2. **Listening:** 84 day-specific official companion assignments with exact title, provider URL, editorial level estimate, focus, task, caption/transcript note, suitability note, rights mode and in-app fallback. There are 28 unique resources in the 84-day sequence, revisited with different listening focuses; the curated catalogue contains 36 named entries. TED-Ed, TED, Huberman Lab and The Diary of a CEO appear in the daily sequence. Joe Rogan Experience is present on the optional discovery shelf only and requires adult preview; it is not compulsory.
3. **Task 1:** 84 original fictional datasets across six visual families (14 each): line graph, bar chart, pie chart, table, process and map. Added six-type teaching guides, exact data inspector, calculated factual summaries/overview prompts, four-section planning board, minimum-150-word reminder and accuracy audit. The checker validates data structure only; it does not grade semantic writing or assign an IELTS band.
4. **Writing Task 2** prompts and the existing day-specific Speaking prompts remain intact.
5. **Rights:** no Barron’s charts/questions/pages are copied. No TED/TED-Ed/podcast media or transcripts are downloaded/re-hosted. Only official provider links are used; the local IELTS Boost MP3s remain original synthetic-voice practice.
6. Deliverables: 84-row listening mapping, 336-row four-skill resource audit, 84-row Task 1 visual audit, catalog JSON and QA report.

## Stage 47 acceptance notes and limitations

- External videos are optional extensions. They do not replace the in-app daily audio, script and question sets.
- CEFR labels are editorial estimates, not provider-certified labels. Durations and captions can change; individual player controls remain authoritative.
- Long-form Huberman/DOAC selections use timecodes where verified; other titles direct learners to the official player and its current duration/captions.
- JRE is optional discovery only and must be previewed by a parent/teacher for age suitability.
- TED/TED-Ed use official pages/links only. TED content must not be edited, truncated, downloaded or transcript-republished; certain structured/commercial curricular uses may require a licence. TED-Ed videos are copyrighted; no downloading, editing or re-uploading.
- Full browser E2E, Samsung A14 microphone/PWA/offline acceptance and actual playback on the user's phone remain outstanding.
