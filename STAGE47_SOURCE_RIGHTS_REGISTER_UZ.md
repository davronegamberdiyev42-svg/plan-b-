# Stage 47 — Resource Rights & Safety Policy

## Core rule

IELTS Boost stores only original learner-practice material (84 synthetic-voice MP3s/scripts, fictional Task 1 datasets, and original prompts) in its local project. External TED/TED-Ed/podcast audio/video/transcripts are not downloaded, edited, cut, copied into the bundle or rehosted. The platform opens the provider’s own official page.

## TED

- Use the official TED talk page/player and provide attribution/title/link. The current TED policy describes non-commercial sharing under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; do not imply TED endorsement. Commercial access, paid course use, or broader redistribution needs a separate rights review/licence.
- Do not edit, shorten, clip, download, re-upload or republish full transcript text as app-owned content.
- TED’s published terms distinguish personal/non-commercial use from commercial or organised educational/course use; some structured curriculum use can require a licence. If distribution context changes, seek rights-holder permission before expanding beyond official link use.

## TED-Ed

- TED-Ed videos are copyrighted and use YouTube standard terms, not the TED Talks CC licence.
- Link to the official TED-Ed lesson or standard official YouTube player/embed where the owner allows it.
- Do not download, edit, trim, subtitle, or re-upload the video.

## Podcasts / YouTube

- Huberman Lab, The Diary of a CEO, and Joe Rogan Experience remain on official provider/official YouTube pages. No podcast MP3s or captions are bundled.
- Current video playback, caption availability, embed permission and age restrictions depend on the provider/platform and can change.
- Huberman health/science material is used as English listening content, not individual medical advice.
- The Diary of a CEO and Joe Rogan are long-form interview shows; an item’s topic and exact segment must be screened before any compulsory use.

## Age suitability

- Joe Rogan Experience is optional discovery only and requires parent/teacher preview. It is not in the mandatory 84-day schedule.
- Where an episode has adult themes or its complete contents have not been reviewed, the platform must not label it age-safe or compulsory.

## Barron’s and other publisher material

- Do not reproduce Barron’s charts, page layouts, question text, answer keys, scans or paid audio.
- Task 1 visuals in this project are original fictional datasets built to practise the same IELTS visual families.
- The 61-article library remains supplementary; any future public distribution must independently verify the article’s copyright and terms.
